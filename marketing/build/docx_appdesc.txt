Learn a language the way you would actually keep doing it — by playing.

LingoQuest teaches 10 languages through short lessons and 10 real word games that use the same vocabulary. Learn a word in a lesson. Play it in a game. Keep it for good.

LEARN → PRACTICE → PLAY
Every lesson takes about five minutes. You meet each word on a flashcard with audio, then work through exercises that ask you to read it, hear it, match it and build it into a sentence. Then the Fun Zone hands those exact words back to you as a game.

10 LANGUAGES, EACH WITH ITS OWN PROGRESS
Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Turkish, Arabic, Japanese and Chinese. Switch whenever you like — every language keeps its own lessons, XP, streak and position on the path, so nothing is lost when you come back.

10 GAMES, ONE VOCABULARY
• Word Bubble — catch the right meaning before it falls
• Word Rush — fast-paced catching with big combos
• Word Match — pair up words and meanings
• Memory Match — flip cards, find the pairs
• Sentence Builder — drag words into the right order
• Phrase Builder — complete everyday phrases
• Listen & Catch — listen closely, catch what you hear
• Meaning Shooter — pop the bubble with the right meaning
• Conversation Challenge — follow a real back-and-forth chat
• Word Survival — answer fast and keep your friend above the rising water

EVERY MISTAKE BECOMES PRACTICE
Get a word wrong and LingoQuest shows you the right answer and what it means, then brings the word back later in the same lesson. After a game, one tap practises only the words you missed.

A CLEAR PATH FORWARD
Units and lessons laid out as a path, so you always know what comes next. A quick placement test at the start means you can skip ahead if you already know some.

PROGRESS YOU CAN SEE
XP levels, day streaks, daily goals, achievement badges, and a vocabulary breakdown that tells you which words you have mastered and which still need work.

LINGOQUEST PRO
Pro unlocks every lesson on the Path and every game in the Fun Zone, with unlimited practice. Monthly and yearly plans are available, and the monthly plan starts with a free trial. Prices are shown in your local currency before you buy.
