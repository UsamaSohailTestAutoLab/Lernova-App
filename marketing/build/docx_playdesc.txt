Learn a language by playing — not by studying.

LingoQuest is a language learning app built around a simple idea: you remember a word when you use it. So every word you meet in a lesson comes back as a game.

Learn Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Turkish, Arabic, Japanese or Chinese. Each language keeps its own lessons, XP and streak, so you can switch whenever you like and pick up exactly where you left off.

━━━ SHORT LESSONS THAT STICK ━━━
Each lesson takes about five minutes. You meet new vocabulary on flashcards with audio, then practise it through several exercise types: multiple choice, listening, typing the meaning, matching words to meanings, arranging words into a sentence, and recognising a word from an image. Get something wrong and the app shows you the correct answer and its meaning, then asks you again before the lesson ends.

━━━ 10 WORD GAMES, ALL BUILT FROM YOUR VOCABULARY ━━━
The Fun Zone is not a separate app bolted on. Every game opens with a quick review of the words it is about to test, and those are the words from your lessons.

Word Bubble — catch the right meaning before it falls
Word Rush — fast-paced word catching with big combos
Word Match — pair up words and meanings
Memory Match — flip cards and find the matching pairs
Sentence Builder — drag words into the right order
Phrase Builder — complete everyday phrases
Listen & Catch — listen closely and catch what you hear
Meaning Shooter — pop the bubble with the right meaning
Conversation Challenge — follow a real back-and-forth chat
Word Survival — answer fast and keep your friend above the rising water

━━━ A PATH, NOT A PILE OF FLASHCARDS ━━━
Lessons are organised into units along a path you can see, so you always know what is next. A quick placement test when you start means you do not have to begin at "hello" if you already know some.

━━━ PRACTICE WHAT YOU ACTUALLY MISSED ━━━
Every lesson ends with a review screen showing what you got wrong, what the right answer was, and what it means — with audio so you can hear it again. After a game, one tap practises only the missed words.

━━━ PROGRESS WORTH COMING BACK FOR ━━━
Earn XP and level up. Build a day streak. Hit a daily goal you set yourself. Unlock achievement badges. Watch your vocabulary breakdown move from weak to learning to mastered.

━━━ LINGOQUEST PRO ━━━
Pro unlocks every lesson on the Path and every game in the Fun Zone, with unlimited practice and fresh questions each time. Monthly and yearly subscriptions are available, and the monthly plan starts with a free trial. All prices are shown in your local currency before purchase and can be cancelled any time in your Google Play account settings.
